F1TENTH Autonomous Racing Car

ROS2 · Python · C++ · Ubuntu VM

This project was a full-scale autonomous driving experience built on the F1TENTH platform, developed as part of a team and tested in a real-time racing environment. Using a virtual machine running Ubuntu with ROS2 Foxy, we designed and developed a complete self-driving stack, including custom ROS2 packages, launch files, and nodes implemented with both Python and C++ . We implemented perception and control systems using LiDAR and a camera, enabling the car to navigate complex tracks fully autonomously. Our system supported real-time obstacle avoidance, emergency braking, and logic to control speed based on a variety of factors. For visual perception, we trained a YOLO model to recognize real-world traffic signs like stop signs, turn signs, and speed limit signs, and built logic to respond accordingly with full stops, speed adjustments, and U-turns, mimicking behavior of real-world autonomous vehicles. We even competed in a head-to-head tournament against 9 other F1TENTH cars, where our team's car came out on top. This challenge forced us to adapt in real-time, trying to find any optimizations possible when we realized how competitive the other teams were.
